## Scoring Rationale

- **cash_flow_fit (55)**: $107k EBITDA is below $140k target by $33k. Negotiating price down to ~$300k would bring effective yield closer to target. Score docked heavily for this gap.
- **operator_fit (70)**: Buyer's skills in PM, client communication fit the sales-and-oversight role well. No cleaning ops background needed given manager.
- **geography_fit (90)**: North Metro Atlanta is the buyer's home market. Excellent.
- **industry_fit (75)**: Commercial cleaning is on the buyer's open-to list. B2B recurring model fits well. Not trade-licensed.
- **deal_structure_fit (60)**: SBA potentially available; seller financing unknown; franchise transfer terms unknown.
- **diligence_risk (60)**: Moderate — franchisor name/terms is the key unknown; everything else is relatively disclosed.

## hard_gates Note

`target_owner_profit: FAIL` — this listing cannot go to top-5 in its current form. It is listed as "high-fit-but-gated" pending either:
(a) price negotiation to bring effective yield to ≥$140k, or
(b) SDE restatement showing true owner CF is higher than disclosed.
