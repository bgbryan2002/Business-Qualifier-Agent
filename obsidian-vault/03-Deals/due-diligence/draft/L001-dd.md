## Top 5 Facts That Change the Buyer's Read

1. **Owner-operator family business.** The named individuals in customer testimonials are the same as the seller — this is a key-person risk that may be terminal for a semi-absentee buyer.
2. **No SDE disclosed.** The entire financial case is unknown. This is the single most important data point needed.
3. **Geography is a hard fail.** Walker County is outside any reasonable commute from Atlanta. Rossville is effectively a Chattanooga-market business.
4. **Dual model (laundromat + pickup/delivery) adds complexity.** Two revenue streams means two operational models to manage remotely.
5. **Real operating business confirmed.** Website is live, coverage area is defined, equipment list is specific — this is not a teaser-only fiction.

## Recommended Next Step

**Pass unless SDE is disclosed and exceeds $150k with a manager pathway.** Geography alone is borderline for this buyer. If seller can demonstrate the P&D route runs with minimal owner involvement, revisit. Otherwise, deprioritize.
