# Acquisition Memo — L004
## Commercial Kitchen Gasket Replacement Service, Savannah GA

### Opening

A Savannah-based B2B route service business — commercial refrigeration gasket replacement for restaurants and foodservice clients — offered at $314,900 with $294,861 in stated annual cash flow. The 1.07x SDE multiple is the most anomalous pricing in the discovery set. This business either has a hidden reason for the low price (health of owner, lease expiration, client concentration) or represents an extraordinary deal. Clarifying which requires a single conversation with the seller.

### Why It Scores Well

- **CF ratio is the best in the set**: If the $294k figure holds at face value (i.e., represents true discretionary earnings to a new owner), this is a 94% return on equity in year one. No comparable opportunity exists in this discovery set.
- **B2B recurring model**: Commercial kitchen equipment requires regular gasket maintenance — this is not project work, it is recurring service. Every new restaurant client is a recurring account. The model is defensible and scalable.
- **No trade license required**: The buyer does not need a HVAC, electrical, or plumbing license. Gasket replacement is a skilled technician task that can be hired, not a licensed-contractor model.
- **Established 2015**: 10-year track record in a B2B maintenance niche. This is not a startup.
- **SBA-eligible structure**: $314k asking is well within SBA 7(a) range.

### Open Questions / Diligence Gaps

1. **Why is the price 1x SDE?** This is the single most important question. A rational seller does not leave $600k–$900k in value on the table without a reason. Ask: health/capacity, forced timeline, partnership dispute, lease expiration, or client concentration?
2. **Is the $294k CF "real"?** This is a ~49% net margin on $603k revenue with 4 employees. Request full SDE addback worksheet — what is the owner paying themselves, what is depreciated, what is personal expense?
3. **Client concentration**: 4 employees on $603k revenue suggests dense route density. Who are the top 3 clients and what % of revenue do they represent?
4. **Is this a Gasket Guy franchise or independent?** Gasket Guy (gasketguy.com) operates in Savannah. If the seller IS a Gasket Guy franchisee, there are franchise transfer terms. If they are independent, there is a competitive risk.
5. **Geography**: Savannah is 4 hours from Atlanta. For a 12-hr/wk buyer, remote management requires a credible manager pathway — either promoting an existing technician or hiring within the first 90 days.

### Recommended Next Step

**PASS** (user decision, 2026-05-15).

The original recommendation was REQUEST-NDA on the basis that the 1.07× SDE multiple was unusually attractive and worth one seller conversation. Buyer-001 passed at GATE 3 review citing geography: Savannah is 4 hours from Atlanta, and the 12-hr/wk semi-absentee profile cannot reliably operate a remote business without a credible local manager already in place. The price anomaly was not strong enough to override the operator-fit constraint.

Listing closed out as `status: rejected`, reason `geography-fail`. No NDA requested. Not pursued further.
