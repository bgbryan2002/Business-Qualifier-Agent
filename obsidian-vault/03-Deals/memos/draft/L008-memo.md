# Acquisition Memo — L008
## Laundromat, Duluth GA (Gwinnett County)

**CAVEAT: Google search snippet data only. BizBuySell returned HTTP 403. Verify before action.**

---

### Opening

A Gwinnett County laundromat with approximately $240,000 annual cash flow at $450,000 asking — if verified, this business significantly exceeds buyer-001's CF target, offers strong debt-service coverage, and sits in one of Atlanta's highest-density suburban markets.

### Why It Scores Well

- **Geography**: Duluth is ~30 minutes NE of Atlanta (I-85 corridor, Gwinnett County). This is within the buyer's commute window.
- **CF headroom for debt service**: $240k CF vs. $140k target gives a $100k buffer. At a $450k purchase price with standard SBA stack (10% seller note $45k + 10% SBA injection $45k + 80% SBA loan $360k), monthly debt service is ~$4,900 ($58,800/year). Net buyer CF: $240k - $58.8k = $181k — 30% above the $140k target.
- **Gwinnett County demographics**: Duluth has one of the highest Korean-American and diverse immigrant population densities in Georgia. These demographics are consistently associated with high laundromat utilization rates, making demand predictable.
- **Laundromat model**: Semi-absentee by design. Coin/card-op machines, no expertise required.

### Open Questions / Diligence Gaps

1. **Why is the multiple low (1.88x)?** Normal laundromats trade at 2.5x–3.5x SDE. The 1.88x implies either: lease expiration within 2-3 years, aging equipment (major capex pending), or declining revenue trend. This is the pivotal question.
2. **Equipment age**: If machines are >10 years old, a full replace may cost $150k–$300k within 3-5 years, destroying the CF thesis.
3. **Lease remaining term**: Critical. A laundromat without 5+ years on its lease (with renewals) has limited value and limited SBA eligibility.
4. **Revenue figure**: $240k CF on what revenue base? If revenue is $400k-$600k, margins are reasonable. If revenue is $250k-$300k, margins are unsustainably high and must be scrutinized.
5. **Manager or attendant**: Is there any existing staff?

### Recommended Next Step

**REQUEST-NDA** (after manual BizBuySell verification). Navigate to BizBuySell.com, Georgia/Atlanta Metro laundromats, Gwinnett County filter. Confirm the listing and financials. If lease is >5 years and equipment is <8 years, this is a strong LOI candidate. The below-market multiple is the first thing to ask the broker about — the answer will either reveal a manageable issue or a deal-breaker.
