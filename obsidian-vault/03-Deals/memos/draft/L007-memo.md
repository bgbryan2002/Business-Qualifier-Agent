## Laundromat, Powder Springs GA (Cobb County)

### Opening

A Cobb County coin laundromat with approximately $140,000 annual cash flow offered at $360,000 — if verified, this is the strongest geographic and financial fit for buyer-001 in the current discovery set: 30 minutes from Atlanta, asking price within SBA range, and CF hitting exactly at the buyer's target floor.

### Why It Scores Well

- **Geography (best in set for laundromats)**: Powder Springs / Cobb County is ~30 minutes west of Atlanta. This is within the buyer's 45-min commute window, enabling regular check-ins without a day-trip commitment.
- **Price-to-CF alignment**: $360k asking / $140k CF = 2.57x SDE multiple. This is the lower range for a laundromat (market is 2.5x–3.5x), meaning the price is not inflated — either it's fair value or there's a mild operational issue to negotiate through.
- **SBA-financeable stack**: A $360k purchase with 10-15% seller note ($36k-$54k) + 10% SBA required injection ($36k) + 80% SBA 7(a) loan ($288k) requires ~$72k in buyer cash. With parental support or small seller carryback, this is structurable even at buyer's $22k liquid. Total monthly debt service at 10.5% / 10-year = ~$3,900/month = $46,800/year, leaving $93k net to the buyer — above minimum.
- **Laundromat model matches semi-absentee intent**: Coin-operated, POS-automated laundromats are inherently low-touch operationally. If payment is card/PayRange, owner check-in is a few hours per week.

### Open Questions / Diligence Gaps

1. **Revenue unknown**: The $140k CF means nothing without knowing gross revenue — high CF on low revenue means higher margin risk; low CF on $300k+ revenue means more resilience.
2. **Manager or attendant in place**: Is there a shift attendant who can serve as a light manager? Or is this a fully unattended facility? (Unattended = higher risk of machine vandalism / theft.)
3. **Lease terms**: What is the remaining lease term before renewal? A laundromat with <5 years left on lease requires careful negotiation — machines are immovable assets.
4. **Equipment age and condition**: What are the machine ages? End-of-life machines create a deferred capex bomb that destroys the CF thesis.
5. **Why is the multiple at the low end?**: 2.57x for an Atlanta-suburb laundromat is below average. Seller motivation and any physical plant issues should be understood before LOI.
